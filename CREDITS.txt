Current character outputs: assets/hero-animated.glb and assets/rival-animated.glb.
Each output contains 18 joints and 18 animation clips (UAL1: 12; UAL2: 6).
Current GLB structure checks passed: 18 joints, 18 named clips and 918 animation channels per actor. Current Chrome selected jump/air and crouch attacks were exercised. Arm-raised frames were reviewed after smoothing 2,361 source vertices; all-pose naturalness and zero cloth penetration are not asserted. The exported Idle is an alternate; the current upright ready stance is a procedural pose.
CC0 applies to the source animation data; the project's generated character geometry/materials are separately sourced, not reclassified as CC0.
The older assets/hero.glb, rival.glb and hero-next.glb are retained recovery exports and are not the engine's active character load targets.
