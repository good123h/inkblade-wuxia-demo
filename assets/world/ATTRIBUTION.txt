ORIGINAL GENERATED ASSET - NOT COVERED BY KENNEY CC0
mountain-gate.glb
Generated for this project using Hunyuan; cleaned, simplified and exported in Blender.
14,000 triangles; embedded 1024 texture; no external image dependency.
Actual POSITION-buffer bounds verified: 8.895132 m wide x 6.0 m high x 8.527417 m deep.
Placed at 7.5 m high in the scene, with its embedded texture retained and tinted for the rain-night palette.
This is an original project-generated asset, not a Kenney asset and not represented as CC0.

Runtime treatment: real GLB geometry is loaded, combined per model, instanced, scaled and recolored in muted ink teal. Original local source files were not edited. The river plane, reflection strokes and distant light points are original procedural scenery. Exterior scenery is not an explorable-world claim.
